## What is a panel of AI agents?

A panel applies separate instructions or roles to the same evidence and compares their judgments. In this app the role-based judgments are authored in Codex using the separate skills and saved as memos; the app does not launch autonomous agents. The evidence and arithmetic are shared, while the questions and thresholds differ. Therefore agreement is correlated evidence rather than independent confirmation of the facts.

A business can satisfy a cash-quality lens and miss a high-growth lens without a contradiction in the underlying facts.
