## Executive View

KKR is a high-quality, diversified alternative-asset manager whose economics increasingly come from recurring management fees, insurance earnings, and long-duration capital rather than traditional private-equity realizations alone. AUM rose from $504 billion in 2022 to $744 billion in 2025, while Fee Related Earnings (FRE) increased from $2.38 billion in 2023 to $3.71 billion in 2025. By Q2 2026, AUM had reached $796 billion and LTM FRE, Total Operating Earnings, and Adjusted Net Income (ANI) per share were all records for KKR as a public company. [S1][S2][S3][S4]

The quality of growth is attractive: 93% of AUM was perpetual or had at least eight years of duration at inception, fee-paying AUM was $638 billion, and KKR raised $133 billion and invested $104 billion during the twelve months ended June 2026. [S4] The principal concerns are valuation, accounting complexity, dependence on market-sensitive realizations, Global Atlantic's insurance and credit risks, dilution from equity compensation and mandatory convertible preferred shares, and a new $250 million proposed DOJ settlement concerning alleged premerger filing violations. [S3][S4][S7]

**Current call: WATCH–DEFER.** The franchise is performing well, but approximately 19.2× LTM ANI per share and 22.7× LTM FRE per share at the reference price do not provide a sufficient margin of safety for KKR's market, insurance, credit, governance, and accounting risks. [S4][S8]

## 1. What KKR Is

KKR operates through three reporting segments: Asset Management, Insurance, and Strategic Holdings. Asset Management includes private equity, real assets, credit and liquid strategies, capital markets, and principal activities. Insurance is conducted through Global Atlantic, which earns a spread between investment income and policyholder costs. Strategic Holdings owns operating companies intended to generate long-term dividends and appreciation. [S3]

The segments reinforce one another. Global Atlantic and other permanent-capital vehicles provide long-duration assets for KKR to manage; KKR earns management fees on those assets and uses its origination network to source investments; realized gains, insurance earnings, and fees can then be reinvested into new strategies, acquisitions, insurance capital, Strategic Holdings, or repurchases. [S3][S6]

## 3. Why the Thesis Can Work

- **Durable fee base:** Long-duration capital reduces forced selling and improves visibility into management fees; 53% of Q2 2026 AUM was perpetual or had at least 15 years of duration at inception. [S4]

- **Fundraising and deployment engine:** KKR raised $133 billion and deployed $104 billion in the LTM period, while uncalled commitments reached $143 billion at June 2026. This supports future fee activation and performance income. [S4]

- **Diversification:** KKR has meaningful scale across private equity, infrastructure, real estate, private credit, leveraged credit, insurance, capital markets, and Strategic Holdings rather than depending on a single flagship buyout strategy. [S3][S4]

- **Global Atlantic flywheel:** Global Atlantic had $219 billion of AUM and more than 3.5 million policyholders at year-end 2025; its long-duration liabilities provide capital that KKR can invest and manage for fees. [S3]

- **Visible earnings pipeline:** On the Q2 2026 earnings call, management said performance-income-eligible deployment over the most recent five years had more than doubled versus the preceding five years, which it believes provides significant visibility into future performance-related income. [S5]

- **Alignment:** Employees owned approximately 30% of KKR's outstanding common shares on a fully exchanged basis at December 2025, and KKR and its personnel had about $30 billion invested or committed alongside clients. [S3]

---

## 4. What Could Go Wrong

- **Market and realization risk:** Carry and investment income depend on valuations and exits. In 2025, total investing earnings declined 21%, and a $344 million Asian Fund II carried-interest repayment reduced reported realized performance income. [S3]

- **Insurance and credit risk:** Global Atlantic introduces asset-liability matching, surrender, mortality, hedging, credit-spread, reinsurance-counterparty, ratings, and regulatory-capital risks. Management said on the Q2 2026 call that the organic growth outlook is difficult to forecast because liability pricing and demand depend on market conditions. [S3][S5]

- **Accounting complexity:** Consolidated funds, CLOs, variable-interest entities, intersegment fees, insurance liabilities, noncontrolling interests, and unrealized carry make GAAP revenue, cash flow, and debt poor stand-alone measures of shareholder economics. [S3]

- **Dilution and adjusted-metric risk:** FY2025 diluted weighted-average shares were 955.8 million versus 890.3 million basic shares. KKR also excludes substantial equity compensation from its segment measures, and the Series D preferred stock is expected to convert into common shares no later than March 2028. [S3]

- **Capital commitments:** KKR had $10.5 billion of its own unfunded investment commitments at year-end 2025, in addition to capital-markets commitments and the capital required to support Global Atlantic. [S3]

- **Governance and compliance:** On August 26, 2026, the DOJ announced a proposed $250 million settlement over allegations that KKR failed to provide complete and accurate Hart-Scott-Rodino filings for at least 16 transactions. The proposed judgment states that the settlement is not an admission or adjudication, but the size and nature of the allegations create a legitimate compliance concern. [S7]

## 5. Capital Structure and Valuation

The earlier approach of adding all $52.9 billion of consolidated debt to market capitalization was incorrect. At December 2025, KKR reported $49.1 billion of Asset Management and Strategic Holdings debt, but approximately $40.1 billion represented debt of consolidated funds, CLOs, CFEs, and other structures; much of that debt is non-recourse to KKR. KKR separately disclosed $9.37 billion of Asset Management note obligations and $3.77 billion of Global Atlantic debt obligations, with Global Atlantic debt non-recourse to KKR beyond Global Atlantic's assets. [S3]

Because KKR combines an asset manager, an insurer, balance-sheet investments, and operating-company holdings, a sum-of-the-parts or per-share earnings framework is more informative than a conventional consolidated enterprise-value calculation. [S3]

These multiples recognize KKR's growth and recurring-earnings quality, but they leave limited room for weaker fundraising, credit losses, delayed exits, insurance-spread pressure, or additional governance problems. This valuation conclusion is an analyst judgment based on the cited price and company-reported non-GAAP measures, not guidance from KKR. [S4][S8]

## 6. Management Outlook and Recent Evidence

At its 2024 Investor Day, KKR targeted FRE per adjusted share above $4.50 in 2026. The company had already reached $4.68 on an LTM basis by Q2 2026, indicating that this target was achieved early on a trailing basis. [S4][S6]

Management reported record quarterly and LTM FRE, Total Operating Earnings, and ANI per share in Q2 2026. It also reported $34 billion of quarterly fundraising and $24 billion of quarterly deployment. These results support the view that the FY2025 momentum continued into 2026. [S4]

Management did not provide a precise full-year forecast for Global Atlantic's organic growth, explaining that volumes depend on markets and weekly repricing of liabilities. It argued, however, that volatility could reduce competition for assets and improve investment opportunities. [S5]

The August 2026 DOJ settlement is the most material adverse development after Q2 results. The $250 million amount is manageable relative to $5.0 billion of LTM ANI, but the alleged conduct matters more than the direct financial cost because KKR's franchise relies on regulator, investor, and transaction-counterparty trust. This materiality assessment is an analyst inference. [S4][S7]

---

## 7. Current Decision

### **WATCH–DEFER — do not initiate at the current reference price**

KKR's business quality and operating momentum are strong: AUM, FPAUM, FRE, ANI, fundraising, and deployment have continued to grow, and management has already exceeded its stated 2026 FRE-per-share objective on an LTM basis. [S4][S6] The present price, however, values KKR at approximately 19.2× LTM ANI and 22.7× LTM FRE before fully resolving insurance risk, dilution, equity-compensation cost, or the compliance implications of the DOJ settlement. [S3][S4][S7][S8]

**What would change the call:** Initiate if the price falls below roughly $95 while LTM ANI remains at least $5.55 per adjusted share and no material deterioration appears in fundraising, credit performance, Global Atlantic, or compliance—equivalent to about 17× LTM ANI. [S4][S8]
