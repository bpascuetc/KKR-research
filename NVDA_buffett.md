**Verdict: own the business provisionally on the financial screen.** Every displayed year clears the 15% ROE hurdle and has positive owner earnings. Between 2022-01-30 and 2026-01-25, the lab's owner-earnings proxy rises from 8,132 to 96,676. That supports the case for a business producing cash after reported capital spending, although the intervening 3,808 at 2023-01-29 shows that endpoint growth is not uninterrupted growth.

I would not treat these passes as proof of a durable moat, sound management or an attractive purchase price. The proxy subtracts all qualifying capital spending rather than identifying maintenance spending. At 2026-01-25, operating cash flow of 102,718 is below net income of 120,067; positive owner cash does not remove that earnings-to-cash concern. I would investigate its drivers before relying on the broader business judgment.
