**Independent reviewer’s question:** What source-backed reconciliation would demonstrate that KKR’s FY2025 $2.34 diluted EPS understates sustainable common-share earnings enough to justify $100.87 at these qualified peer multiples?

**Answer:** The evidence assembled here does not establish such an understatement. ## 12. Final decisions and short defense

**Student-confirmed decisions:** retain both qualified peers and choose watch–defer. Bernat selected options 1A and 2A after reviewing the explanations in this conversation. The rationale below supports those choices; it is AI-assisted wording, not a verbatim statement by Bernat.

**Why keep both peers?** Each has a meaningful alternative-asset management business. Apollo also brings insurance ownership economics, while Blackstone offers a useful asset-manager comparison with a different insurance relationship. Those differences justify qualification and sensitivity analysis rather than pretending the businesses are identical. Keeping both also avoids choosing a peer merely because it produces a preferred price.

**Why watch–defer rather than declare KKR overvalued?** Both conditional ranges sit below the historical market price, but KKR may deserve a different multiple if its future earnings or business mix justify one. The evidence supports waiting for a stronger explanation, not claiming certainty about fair value.

**Which AI criticism matters most?** Reported GAAP earnings are not automatically comparable measures of sustainable cash generation. The DCF has a related weakness: assumed cash conversion. Accepting that criticism means qualifying the conclusion and seeking a reconciliation, not averaging the models or changing EPS to get a more attractive answer.

**What would change the decision?** A source-backed explanation of sustainable earnings and cash available to common shareholders, with a supported discount rate and dilution treatment, could change the valuation. A lower market price would prompt a review but would not fix weak assumptions by itself.
